# Data and provenance notice

This repository uses no observational or laboratory endpoint data.

The files under `data/derived/` are compact deterministic artifacts generated
by the scripts in `scripts/`. They freeze typed source candidates, theorem
ledgers, interface contracts and adversarial audit results. They are not
measurements and must not be interpreted as empirical evidence for Tau Core.
The P1 ambient-rank artifact is likewise a finite mathematical control, not
an independently measured or Nature-certified physical source algebra.

The paper figure is regenerated by `scripts/build_figures.py`. The PDF and the
source-only arXiv archive are rebuilt by `scripts/reproduce.py`.

No raw downloads, private workbench notes, endpoint-fitted parameters or
terminal residuals are required for reproduction.
