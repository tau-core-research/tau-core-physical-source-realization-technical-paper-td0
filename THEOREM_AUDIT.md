that Nature occupies the selected completion.

## Circularity audit

No terminal map, terminal label, empirical residual, standard comparator or
endpoint score enters the source selector. The role-rotation countermodel
shows explicitly that spectral selection cannot create terminal typing.

## Minimal corrected physical statement

If the physical parent owns the declared complete seed grammar, its Hodge
action unit, the constrained additive spectral-minimum law and a two-sided
typed realization, then the unique source-equivalence class exports a frozen
`BaseSeedPacket`. The present Tau corpus supplies a nonempty conditional
completion and exact falsifiers, but not independent evidence that Nature owns
the variational law or occupies that class.

The P3/CLP3 instantiation now proves that this antecedent is nonempty and
fully executable on one declared finite source support. It does not prove the
antecedent itself for the physical universe.

The sharper result is that, once P1 supplies the complete physical source
language, a single source-fidelity/Gram/body-square action can supply P4--P7
together. P1 is irreducible source data for that law: no functional can prove
the exhaustivity of the metalanguage in which the functional itself is
defined.

P1 is nevertheless conditionally certifiable relative to an independently
fixed finite ambient physical source algebra: faithfulness makes the regular
GNS embedding injective, and full seed-generated GNS rank is then equivalent
to equality with that ambient algebra. The current P3/CLP3 control passes the
finite rank equality but not the independence gate, so physical P1 ownership
remains open.

The pre-body PUSC declaration supplies the first candidate-independent
ambient that can actually be inserted into this theorem. It does not turn the
previous `256/256` control into a physical success. Instead, under the
ontic-envelope and physical-embedding assumptions, it changes the comparison
to `256/1024` and rejects *global* P1 while preserving completeness of the
declared P3 component. Treating the same Clifford envelope as an auxiliary
metric completion avoids that rejection but restores ambient
non-identifiability. Current source laws do not select between these two
interpretations.

The connector theorem replaces the unspecified positive ontic branch by an
explicit finite completion. In the declared `Cl_10` grading, the two
homogeneous connector modes generate the missing off-block algebra and restore
the conditional rank equality `1024/1024`. The accompanying defect action
selects their Clifford zero locus only after those variables and relations are
supplied. This closes the algebraic realization problem but not the physical
source-ownership problem.

The record--Clifford theorem sharpens that last boundary. Once a graded
embedding of the already present binary record algebra into the already
present three-dimensional PUSC complement is supplied, its record involution
forces a `2+1` adjoint split. The earlier two connector generators are only a
choice of frame in this split. Thus separate connector primitives are not
needed. However, explicit same-reduct controls prove that P3, PUSC and record
data without a cross-grading relation do not entail the embedding. Physical
selection and occupation of that one relation remain open.

The landing-orbit theorem reduces the statement still further. Inside the
even relative commutant, a trace-preserving binary-record embedding is
automatically a pair of complementary rank-one projections. Its difference
is a unit bivector, so complement preservation and the `2+1` adjoint split
follow without extra assumptions, and `Spin(3)` makes all representatives
gauge equivalent. The current joint PUSC--record action nonetheless cannot
derive the landing: its variables are only the PUSC coordinates and the
Frobenius record response. No record-to-Clifford map or cross-landing residual
appears in its Euler equations. The remaining physical question is therefore
occupation of one landing class, not a choice among embeddings.

The same-reduct theorem now makes that failure strict rather than merely an
inventory observation. The internal and external record completions agree on
every currently displayed source relation, including the common occupied
coordinate and the Schur-neutral unfolding action, while only the internal
completion lands in the PUSC envelope. A minimal positive extension is also
available: once an in-envelope record involution is independently owned, the
direct sum of its seven role commutators and its grading commutator has the
landing class as its exact zero set. This closes the mathematical form of the
missing relation. It does not close physical ownership of the variable or
Nature occupation of that relation.

The JGR--IGL theorem now retypes the positive completion. In the frozen PUSC
realization, an IGL zero is equivalent to a faithful graded representation of
the already present abstract role--record product in the same ontic envelope.
This removes the interpretation of IGL as an arbitrary new coupling. It does
not remove the physical premise: an external faithful representation shows
that abstract joint-algebra ownership is weaker than same-envelope
internality, while the odd and role-mixing controls show that grading and
tensor locality cannot be dropped.

The JCM theorem supplies a conditional selection mechanism rather than a new
landing law. The joint algebra has four simple size-eight blocks, so its
minimal faithful carrier is 32-dimensional and is already realized by the
internal PUSC packet. Proper same-compression extensions cost more under the
existing unnormalized heat trace. This closes same-envelope selection inside
that combined source packet. It does not prove that the physical source
co-owns the joint grammar and selector; normalized-trace and changed-
compression controls show that neither premise may be suppressed.

The UTR/SPS/HCI results sharpen that last boundary. Ordinary trace, positive
logarithmic sign and common compression no longer need to be inserted as
three unrelated formulas: they follow from direct-sum compatibility,
spectator-stable well-posed minimization and source-Hodge intertwining. The
new no-go is equally important. Joint algebra ownership does not entail the
Hodge intertwiner, and the present source has not been shown to own spectral
sufficiency or the well-posed candidate class. Structural uniqueness is
therefore proved conditionally; physical co-ownership and occupation remain
open.

DHF-P1 now derives HCI from one deeper grammar-level statement. When the
physical star presentation contains the nilpotent differential generator,
full-star representation automatically transports both the forward and
adjoint incidence, hence the Laplacian, harmonic projector and Hodge
operator. This is strictly stronger than a forward chain map: the explicit
same-chain control changes Hodge compression through the missing adjoint
relation. The formula-level Hodge premise is therefore removed, but its
physical source content is not. Current JGR owns role--record algebra, not yet
the differential generator in that same carrier.
